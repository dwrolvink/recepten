# Ingredients
## Dough

* 3/4 cup (180g) warm milk 
* 1 pack (7g) instant yeast 
* 1/4 cup (50g) sugar 
* 1 tsp instant espresso 
* 1 egg + 1 egg yolk 
* 1/4 cup (57g) melted unsalted butter 
* 3 cups (360g) bread flour (patentbloem) 
* 3/4 tsp (4g) salt 

## Filling

* 1⁄2 cup granulated sugar
* 1/2 cup (114g) unsalted butter, softened
* 1 tbsp. ground cinnamon
* 1 1⁄2 tsp. cardamom seeds, finely crushed

# Instructions
## Dough

* Add warm milk into a large bowl with sugar, melted butter, and sprinkle on the yeast. 
* Give it a little mix and then add in the eggs, salt, and instant espresso. 
* Add in flour and knead for about 8-10 minutes and then let rise for an hour or until its doubled in size.

> Note: if you’re using active dry yeast, you’ll need to activate it in warm milk and sugar first before adding the other ingredients. 

## Filling
- Mix granulated sugar, butter, cinnamon, and cardamom in a bowl until smooth.

## Cinnamon rolls

* Roll out the dough and spread mixture on evenly. 
* Roll up and cut into 8 equal pieces. 
* Let rise for another hour
* Bake at 350f for 25-30 min (check them around the 23 min mark)
