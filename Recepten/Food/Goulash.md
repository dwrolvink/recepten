  
# Introduction
*   Feeds 2
  
# Ingredients 

## Total

*   450 grams peeled potatoes    
*   300 grams carrots
*   500 grams chicken breast filet or chicken thigh filet
*   2 big bell peppers
*   2 garlic cloves
*   1 large banana pepper (or hotter)
*   1 egg
*   salt
*   black pepper
*   powdered chicken bouillon
*   onion powder
*   garlic powder
*   dill
*   paprika powder
*   smoked paprika powder (optional)
    
## Potato pancakes

*   450 grams peeled potatoes
*   1 egg
*   0.5 tsp onion powder
*   0.5 tsp garlic powder
*   olive oil as much as needed
*   1 pinch salt

## Goulash

*   300 grams carrots
*   500 grams chicken breast filet or chicken thigh filet
*   500 ml water (about two cups)
*   2 tbsp powdered chicken bouillon
*   2 big bell peppers
*   1 large banana pepper (or hotter)
*   ¼ cup olive oil
*   2 tbsp dill
*   1 tsp black pepper (ground)
*   1 tsp paprika powder
*   1 big pinch smoked paprika powder
*   2 cloves garlic
    

# Instructions
## Potato pancakes

*   Grate the potatoes on a cheese grate A
*   Mix all ingredients together in one bowl 
*   Pour a little bit of olive oil on a pan, put pan on medium heat
*   Put a scoop of potato mix in the pan, and quickly flatten out as much as possible <sup>2</sup>
*   When the edges turn brown, flip the pancake over
*   Add a little bit of extra olive oil in the pan if none is left (helps with the browning)
*   Cook for another minute or two
*   Cover two plates with the pancakes

## Goulash
*   Chop the carrots into thick rings
*   Fill a pan with the water and the powdered chicken bouillon, heat to a boil
*   Boil the carrots for about 6 minutes, or until "al dente".
*   Drain and save the filtrate
*   Finely dice the chicken, sprinkle with black pepper and salt to taste (jerk spices would work well here too)
*   Add part of the olive oil in a large pan, and fry the chicken at high heat until all outsides are white
*   Add the rest of the olive oil, and add the chopped bell peppers
*   Fry for 2 minutes at high heat
*   Add carrots and ½ cup carrot filtrate
*   Heat until boiling, then lower heat to simmer
*   Add 1 tbsp + 1 tsp of dill, 1 tsp of freshly ground black pepper, the paprika powder, chopped banana pepper, and chopped garlic cloves.
*   Simmer / low boil until the sauce is thick (i.e. the volume of sauce has decreased significantly, it doesn't really thicken)
*   Turn off the heat
*   Add the rest of the dill (½ tsp) <sup>3</sup>, and a big pinch of smoked paprika. Salt to taste. _(I normally don't add any salt at this stage, the bouillon and salt from the chicken is often enough)._
*   Stir well, cover and let sit for 15 minutes
*   When ready to eat, pour on top of the pancakes
    
  

Notes
1.  This recipe has been made less hot for Sienna. I used to add about 1 tsp of chilli pepper powder, and 1 red pepper. I like it less hot like this, but I feel like it's more authentic when it's hotter.
    
2.  Mix the potato mix well before every scoop, the juice helps bind the potato shavings and this juice settles to the bottom really quickly. Flattening the pancakes works well by tapping the pancake with the backside of a tablespoon. It's an acquired skill, but it pays off (flatter = better taste).
    
3.  Normally I'd use actual tea spoons to measure recipes, I used Sienna's measuring set to make this recipe and a teaspoon with those is actually **way** more. Anyways, in the cooking phase, you should add lots of dill, the high notes get boiled away and the large amount of simmered dill gives this dish its characteristic body. The dill at the end is to add back the high notes that were boiled out.
